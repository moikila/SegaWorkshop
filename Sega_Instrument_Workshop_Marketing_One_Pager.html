Feel the Heartbeat of Sega

Experience Mauritian Sega music in a fun, hands-on 30-minute workshop. Learn the shared heartbeat, explore traditional rhythms and perform together as an ensemble. No musical experience or knowledge of Mauritian Creole is required.

What you'll learn

  • Feel and play the basic Sega rhythm - the Heartbeat of Sega.
  • Build rhythm through body percussion, wooden sticks and vocal calls.
  • Learn a simple ravanne, djembe or supporting-instrument part.
  • Listen for shared cues and play together as a group.
  • Discover Mauritian instruments, music and cultural traditions.

Your 30-minute workshop

  1. Meet and discover - Meet the instructors and instruments.
  2. Feel the heartbeat - Use body percussion, thigh pats or claps.
  3. Pick up the sticks - Transfer the heartbeat to rhythm sticks.
  4. Sing and respond - Add an easy vocal call-and-response.
  5. Learn your part - Join a small instrument group.
  6. Perform together - Combine every part for a final performance.
